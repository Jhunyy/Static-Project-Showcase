Our Research Team

The passionate minds behind this innovation

Meet the Researchers

Behind this groundbreaking innovation is a dedicated team of researchers, developers, and designers committed to making mobile communication safer for everyone. Our diverse backgrounds in cybersecurity, data science, and user experience design come together to create a comprehensive solution to the growing problem of smishing attacks.

Each team member brings unique expertise and perspective to the project, ensuring our solution is not only technically sound but also user-friendly and accessible to all demographics.

Jhunren Apiag

Lead Researcher

As the team's lead developer, Jhunren focuses on building and testing the core smishing detection feature of the project. He applies his knowledge in mobile development and basic machine learning to design the system's keyword-based detection algorithm. His goal is to make the app both functional and reliable for everyday users.

Python Java UI/UX design

Gio Patrick Cimeni

Data Analyst

Gio handles the data gathering and analysis part of the project. He works on collecting sample SMS messages, identifying common scam keywords, and testing how the system detects them. His skills in statistics and data interpretation help the group refine the accuracy of their detection method.

Data Analysis Statistics R Tableau

Edison Dasok

UI/UX Designer

Edison ensures our solution is intuitive and accessible to users of all ages and technical abilities. His user-centered design approach focuses on creating clear, actionable alerts and an overall positive user experience.

UI Design UX Research Prototyping Figma
